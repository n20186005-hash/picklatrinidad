# Research and media sources

This project intentionally separates source notes from visitor-facing copy. Operational details such as hours, picking prices and availability can change; the page describes variable items as estimates or advises visitors to confirm on arrival.

## Place and travel research

- Google Maps listing supplied by the project brief for the exact place name, coordinates, address context, rating and map destination.
- La Trinidad Tourism / Municipality tourism material for local destination context.
- Benguet State University and Philippine News Agency coverage for the Strawberry Farm / Betag agricultural-tourism context.
- Current public visitor listings and local travel references were cross-checked for typical opening, access, transport and seasonal-picking guidance; volatile prices are not presented as guaranteed fixed tariffs.

## Climate, water and ecology

- Real-time conditions and the 7-day outlook come from a public weather service; readings are cached briefly in the visitor’s browser, and the page never displays provider, plan or key details.
- The seasonal strategy table and the climate briefing use long-term Philippine highland climate averages — dry November–May, wet June–October, cool upland temperatures — treated as a “typical year” reference rather than a forecast for any specific day.
- Water and ecology notes describe the valley’s irrigation and farmland habitat in general terms; precise figures should be taken from the local meteorological and hydrological authorities.
